Sound effects in assets/sounds/sfx/ were cut, layered, pitched and levelled
from these packs. Every one is CC0 (public domain): no attribution is
required, but the authors are credited here with thanks.

Impact Sounds (Kenney) -- CC0
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  punches, soft impacts, wood, metal, glass, bells and mining hits.

RPG Audio (Kenney) -- CC0
  https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
  knife slices and chops.

Basic Spell Impacts (Lentikula) -- CC0
  https://lentikula.itch.io/freecc0-basic-spell-impacts-sfx
  fire, ice, lightning and water spell impacts.

Impact (Iwan 'qubodup' Gabovitch) -- CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/impact
  wet meat and stone impacts.

2 wooden squish splatter sequences (Independent.nu, posted by qubodup) -- CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/2-wooden-squish-splatter-sequences
  the splatter in the gory critical hits.

20 sword sound effects (StarNinjas) -- CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes
  sword swings, and the whoosh of a dodged blow.

80 CC0 creature SFX (rubberduck) -- CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx
  the monster voices: grunts, roars, hisses, screams.

The families: slash, pierce, blunt (weapons); fire, frost, lightning, water,
arcane, shadow, light, earth, rot, blood (spells); crit_gore, crit_bone,
crit_fire, crit_frost, crit_storm, crit_magic (the extra layer a critical
hit adds); dodge. Five variants each. Also levelup and death (the
player's), and voice_<kind>_<alert|hurt|death> for each kind of monster:
goblin, brute, beast, spider, bones, spirit, human, drowned.

Rebuild them all with tools/sfx_build.py (set SFX_SRC to the unpacked packs).
