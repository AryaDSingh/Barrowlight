Sound effects in assets/sounds/sfx/ were cut, layered, pitched and levelled
from these packs. All are CC0 (public domain) except Michel Baradari's,
which is CC-BY 3.0 and credited as its license requires; the CC0 authors
are credited here with thanks.

11 male human pain/death sounds, by Michel Baradari -- CC-BY 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/11-male-human-paindeath-sounds
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  human cries of pain and death (used as voice_human_hurt and _death).

The families: slash, pierce, blunt (weapons); fire, frost, lightning, water,
arcane, shadow, light, earth, rot, blood (spells); crit_gore, crit_bone,
crit_fire, crit_frost, crit_storm, crit_magic (the extra layer a critical
hit adds); dodge. Five variants each. Also levelup and death (the
player's), and voice_<kind>_<alert|hurt|death> for each kind of monster:
goblin, brute, beast, spider, bones, spirit, human, drowned.

Rebuild them all with tools/sfx_build.py (set SFX_SRC to the unpacked packs).
